# 角色描述參考格式

## 標準角色描述模板

```
Character reference sheet, [族裔] [性別], approximately [年齡] years old,
[職業], [體型] build, [姿態],
[眼鏡描述（若有）],
[髮型描述],
wearing [上衣], [下著], [鞋子],
[配件],
[表情/氣質描述],
clean neutral background, full body and face close-up side by side,
character design reference, flat soft lighting, 16:9
```

---

## 各欄位常用英文詞彙

### 族裔
- `East Asian`
- `Taiwanese`

### 年齡描述
- `approximately 22-25 years old`
- `in his/her late 20s`
- `around 30`

### 體型
- `slim build`
- `petite cute figure`
- `average build`
- `athletic build`

### 姿態
- `slightly slouched relaxed posture`（工程師感）
- `upright confident posture`（行銷/業務感）
- `casual relaxed stance`

### 眼鏡
- `square metal-framed glasses`（方形金屬框）
- `round thin-framed glasses`（圓形細框）
- `thick-framed black glasses`（粗黑框）
- `rimless glasses`（無框）

### 髮型
| 中文 | 英文 |
|------|------|
| 短鮑伯頭 | `short bob haircut` |
| 淡棕染色 | `with light brown highlights` |
| 微亂中長髮 | `medium-length slightly tousled dark hair` |
| 俐落短髮 | `neat short hair` |
| 馬尾 | `hair tied in a ponytail` |
| 瀏海 | `bangs falling over forehead` |

### 服裝風格
| 風格 | 英文 |
|------|------|
| 工程師休閒 | `blue-grey plaid flannel shirt over dark t-shirt, dark slim jeans` |
| 行銷活潑 | `navy varsity jacket over white hoodie, navy sweatpants` |
| 辦公室休閒 | `simple office casual wear` |
| 文青 | `oversized beige cardigan, wide-leg trousers` |

### 鞋子
- `grey Vans canvas shoes`
- `white New Balance sneakers`
- `simple white sneakers`
- `black leather oxford shoes`

### 配件
- `simple wristwatch`
- `small stud earrings`
- `navy baseball cap with small white text`
- `canvas tote bag with patches`

### 表情氣質
| 個性 | 英文描述 |
|------|---------|
| 內向工程師 | `quiet and introverted facial expression, deep observant eyes, slightly unshaven jawline` |
| 開朗行銷 | `warm natural bright smile, cheerful sparkling lively eyes, friendly approachable demeanor` |
| 文青藝術家 | `dreamy distant gaze, soft melancholic expression` |
| 自信業務 | `confident warm smile, direct eye contact` |

## 場景快速字串格式

場景快速字串是濃縮版的角色描述，用於每個分鏡提示詞中：

```
[族裔] [性別] [職業] [年齡描述], [體型], [眼鏡], [髮型], [主要服裝], [氣質描述]
```

### 範例
```
young East Asian male engineer in his late 20s, slim build,
square metal-framed glasses, medium-length slightly tousled dark hair,
blue-grey plaid flannel shirt and dark jeans,
quiet introverted expression, deep observant eyes behind square glasses, slightly unshaven
```

```
young East Asian female marketing worker around 30, petite cute figure,
short light-brown-highlighted bob haircut, navy varsity jacket or casual outfit,
warm natural bright smile, cheerful sparkling eyes, small stud earrings, lively outgoing energy
```
